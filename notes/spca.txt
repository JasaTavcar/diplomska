2 BACKGROUND AND RELATED WORK

PCA:
L holds the top r principal components (directions) as vectors
VE tells us the % of the variance captured in projected space

SPCA:
Dimensions with most variance does not equal most predictive dimensions for Y

Approaches:
    * Fisher's discriminant analysis, FDA:
        minimizes intraclass variance, maximizes interclass variance
    * Partial least squares, PLS
        projects both X and Y to determine their relationship
    * Reduce ranked regression, RRR
        assumes relationship (projection matrix) between X and Y is low rank
    * Bair's method (oldest, two steps):
        1. univariate feature selection (weights, same as feature selection?)
        2. PCA on selected features
        drawback: only optimizes for indvidually predictive features
    * Goes on to explain other methods, will come back if relevant

3 APPROACH

Pareto optimality:
If goals are exclusive (minimizing PE and maximizing VE are), Pareto fronier
defines a function (epigraph) in the performance measure space that describes
available best performances on the problem.

We will optimize a weighted sum of performance measures.

Since the multiobjective loss function will not be solvable by SVD,
we need to enforse orthonormality of L by other means - manifold gradient algorithms
